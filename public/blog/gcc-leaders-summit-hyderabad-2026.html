GCC Leaders Summit in Hyderabad: GCC E², 30 October 2026

GCC E², GCC Ecosystem Empower, is an invitation-only, full-day summit for Global Capability Centre leaders and ecosystem partners. It takes place in person at HY-ONE Convention in Hyderabad, Telangana, India.

Title Sponsor: State Bank Of India.

Who is the summit for?

The published audience includes GCC CXOs, VPs and transformation leaders; technology, services and solution providers; investors; talent and capability partners; policymakers, academia and industry bodies.

The summit is framed around leadership, ecosystem and opportunity: strategy, scale and capability; technology, capital, talent and partnerships; and conversations that can become action.

Use the official invitation application. Review the current delegate fee on the registration page and follow the instructions for your application. An application alone does not confirm admission or payment.
